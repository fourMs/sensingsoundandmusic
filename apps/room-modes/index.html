Room modes

Between two parallel walls, sound waves whose half-wavelength fits a whole number of times build up standing waves called room modes. At those frequencies some spots in the room boom and others fall almost silent. Enter the size of your own room, see where its axial modes land, and use the hear buttons to hunt the boom by walking around while a mode plays.

Room size

What to notice: modes from different dimensions that land within a few hertz of each other (flagged in the table) pile up into pronounced trouble spots. Real rooms also have tangential and oblique modes between the walls, floor, and ceiling, and absorption shifts and softens everything; the axial modes computed here are the first approximation.
