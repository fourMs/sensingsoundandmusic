Gesture-controlled sound

Hand synth

Move your hands to play a continuous tone. A webcam and MediaPipe track both hands: pinch your right thumb and index fingertip to change the loudness, then move your hands apart or together to sweep the pitch.

Your camera image is processed in this browser and is never recorded or uploaded. On first use, the MediaPipe code, WebAssembly files, and hand model are downloaded from jsDelivr and Google to your device.

Allow camera access to begin. For camera access, open this page over HTTPS or localhost.
