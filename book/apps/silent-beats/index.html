Silent beats

A pulse does not have to be played to be heard. This page loops one bar of 4/4 with two tracks: a pulse of four clicks and a rhythm on a grid of sixteenths. Silence any click or any event, let the pulse drop out for whole bars, or rotate the rhythm against the beat, and listen to what your sense of the pulse does. Nothing is measured; the experiment is in your ears.
