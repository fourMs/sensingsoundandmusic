# Course schedule and weekly matrix

The course is organised for **12 teaching weeks** with **2 hours of in-class work** per week. You should plan roughly **6 hours of reading and assignments** outside class each week (including longer tasks near exam or project deadlines, as announced on the course site).

| Week | Chapter | Focus (what to take away) | Suggested independent work (~6h) | In-class (theory) | In-class or home (applied) |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tuning in | Sensing, sound, music; interdisciplinarity; how this course is structured | 2h reading; 1h note-taking; 1h tools exploration; 2h reflection / short post | Course goals, fields, disciplinarities | Glossary: define *sensing*, *sound*, *music* in your own words; try one AI or app from the intro list reflectively |
| 2 | Listening | Soundscape, ways of describing sound, artists and methods | 2.5h reading; 1.5h listening exercises; 2h written reflection | Sound studies vs acoustics vs psychoacoustics | Two-minute closed-eyes listen; short sound journal entry |
| 3 | Acoustics | Waves, spectra, rooms, instruments—physical causes of what we hear | 2h reading; 2h exercises; 2h concept map | Cause → propagation → reception chain | Optional: Sonic Visualiser or notebook plots on a sound you choose |
| 4 | Psychoacoustics | Ear, loudness, pitch, masking, illusions—physics to perception | 2.5h reading; 2h exercises; 1.5h revision | Thresholds, critical bands, streaming | Demo predictions for masking / continuity illusion |
| 5 | Electroacoustics | Capture, conversion, digital audio, monitors—technology chain | 2h reading; 2h signal-chain diagram; 2h exercises | AD/DA, microphones, loudspeakers | Sketch a recording chain for one of your own setups |
| 6 | Time and rhythm | Meter, groove, micro-timing, entrainment | 2h reading; 2h listening analysis; 2h tapping / annotation | Pulse vs meter; groove vocabulary | Tap-along or Maître Gnome–style timing capture if available |
| 7 | Harmony and melody | Spectral and symbolic views; expectation and structure | 2h reading; 3h analysis tasks; 1h revision | Scales, chords, chroma / symbolic overlap | Short harmonic or melodic annotation of a chosen excerpt |
| 8 | The body | Embodiment, performance gestures, motion capture ideas | 2h reading; 2h observation; 2h short essay plan | Performer vs perceiver; embodied cognition | Record or describe body motion while listening or performing |
| 9 | Physiology | Autonomic responses; HR, HRV, EDA, breathing; measurement ethics | 2.5h reading; 2h exercises; 1.5h concept links | Sympathetic/parasympathetic; what each signal reflects | Relate one physiological idea to your own listening experience |
| 10 | The brain | Regions, pathways, cognition, emotion networks; neuro methods | 2.5h reading; 2h comparison tables (EEG/fMRI…); 1.5h revision | Methods trade-offs | Pick one finding; identify claim–evidence–method |
| 11 | Vision | Audiovisual integration; eye tracking; multimedia | 2h reading; 2h experiments / demos; 2h short analysis | AV vs audio-video; selective attention | Short clip: note where you look vs what you hear |
| 12 | Machine listening | Features, MIR, ML; human vs machine; **data and AI ethics** | 2h reading; 3h notebook / tool exercises; 1h ethics reflection | Pipeline: audio → features → task | Run or inspect one feature plot; discuss bias/dataset limits |

## Using this matrix

- **Theory column**: concepts to rehearse for exams and for reading empirical papers (see *Reading empirical research* on the introduction page).
- **Applied column**: keeps the “musicology + psychology + technology” balance—listening, analysis, or light computation depending on the week.
- **Heavy chapters** (e.g. harmony and melody, machine listening): treat subsection headings as **essential vs optional depth** if time is tight; your instructor may flag priority sections each week.

## After week 12

- Use **General comments** (appendix) for academic writing expectations on essays and the exam.
- Revisit the **introduction** learning outcomes when preparing your exam revision plan.
